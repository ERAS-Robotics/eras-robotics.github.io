How to Contribute

All contributions must be original and not previously published or currently under review elsewhere.
They must be in IEEE RAS standard format for conferences.
Accepted papers will be included in the proceedings and published in IEEE xplore. For inclusion in the proceedings, at least one author will need to register for the conference.
Download the Call for Papers

The submission system is open for final submission! Please upload your submissions in PaperPlaza
Final contribution deadline 15 May 2026!

 

Submission Categories

Contributions addressing aspects of reliability across the entire life cycle of autonomous systems are welcome. We accept both short/position papers (4 pages including references, with the possibility to purchase 1 extra page) and regular papers (6 pages including references, with the possibility to purchase up to 2 extra pages). We also accept late breaking reports with new preliminary ideas.

We welcome submissions in the following categories:

Paper Submissions:

6 pages including references, with the possibility to purchase up to 2 extra pages

Short/Position Paper Submissions:

4 pages including references, with the possibility to purchase 1 extra page

Workshop/Tutorial/Competition Proposal Submissions:

Max 6 pages including references, describing the proposed workshop/tutorial/competition, its objectives, format, and relevance to the conference themes.

Late Breaking Report Submissions:

Extended abstracts in IEEE RAS standard format for conferences. Submissions must be in PDF format and should not exceed two pages, excluding references. The accepted abstracts will be showcased as a poster presentation.
They will not be included in the proceedings.

r-articles Submissions:

You can submit r-articles, see here:  :https://www.ieee-ras.org/publications/ram/information-for-authors/reproducible-articles-r-articles-short-replication-articles-r-articles-reply-articles/   and here: https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-025-01114-7.. This is the only conference where you can do it. For further information about r-articles please refer to the Call for Papers.

r-articles are up to 5 pages, you can pay an extra page.

All papers will undergo a DOUBLE-BLIND review (check IEEE policy on https://www.ieee-ras.org/publications/rules-for-the-double-anonymous-review-process);>>>>>>>the conference proceedings will be published on IEEExplore <<<<<<< (both regular and short/position papers; late breaking reports are excluded from proceedings).
