July 19-20, 2027
Halifax, Canada

Welcome to the official site of the ERAS 2027 Conference.

2027 Engineering Reliable Autonomous Systems will be a premier event dedicated to advancing the fields of AI, Robotics and autonomous systems. This conference will bring together leading researchers, practitioners, and industry experts to share their latest findings, innovations, and insights in the realm of autonomous systems. Join us at the Dalhousie University for two days of engaging presentations, discussions, and networking opportunities that will shape the future of autonomous systems technology.

The venue will be the Auditorium at the Dalhousie University, 6283 Alumni Crescent, Halifax, Canada. 

It’s time for intelligent robots to go mainstream. Join us to remove the scientific and engineering bottlenecks on the way to reliable and ubiquitous autonomus systems.

ERAS 2027 will be the third edition of the ERAS conference series.

Registration

The registration system will be open soon.

All the papers accepted for presentation at the conference will be included in the conference proceedings and included in IEEE Xplore.For inclusion in the proceedings, at least one author will need to register for the conference.
